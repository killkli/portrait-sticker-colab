# 表情貼圖風格庫與 Prompt 指引 (Sticker Styles Reference)

本文件定義 `portrait-sticker-maker` 支援的 6 大核心貼圖風格與對應的視覺特徵、Prompt 提示詞配方。

---

## 1. 日系 Q 版萌系 (Chibi Anime)
- **視覺特徵**：2頭身比例、大頭小身、圓潤流暢向量線條、大眼高光、粉嫩腮紅、賽璐珞賽璐風格（Cell Shading）。類似卡娜赫拉、白爛貓、動漫周邊 Q 版立牌。
- **Prompt 核心關鍵字**：
  ```text
  Ultra-cute 2D Chibi anime character sticker design, based on the facial features, hairstyle, and likeness of the person in <Picture 1>. 2-head-tall proportions, adorable oversized head, cute expressive anime eyes, soft rosy blush, clean bold outlines, vibrant cell shading, die-cut sticker aesthetics.
  ```

---

## 2. 3D 盲盒公仔風 (Pop Mart / Clay 3D)
- **視覺特徵**：潮玩手辦質感（如泡泡瑪特 Molly/Dimoo 質感）、細膩軟陶黏土光澤、柔光箱棚拍立體光影、陶瓷或矽膠微反光。
- **Prompt 核心關鍵字**：
  ```text
  Trendy 3D vinyl toy figurine sticker, Pop Mart style, blind box collectible character, faithfully stylized based on the person in <Picture 1>. Smooth soft clay texture, warm studio softbox lighting, glossy eye reflection, miniature chibi toy proportions, clean physical die-cut sticker look.
  ```

---

## 3. 美式復古漫畫 / 普普風 (Retro Comic / Pop-Art)
- **視覺特徵**：1960年代美漫普普風（Roy Lichtenstein 感）、粗黑重墨線、班戴網點（Ben-Day dots）陰影、高對比飽和色彩、狀聲詞爆炸框（BOOM! WOW!）。
- **Prompt 核心關鍵字**：
  ```text
  Vintage American comic book sticker illustration, 1960s pop-art Lichtenstein style, stylized portrait based on the person in <Picture 1>. Heavy bold ink outlines, halftone Ben-Day dots shading, bold primary color palette, dynamic retro comic speech bubbles, sticker graphic aesthetics.
  ```

---

## 4. 經典 2D 像素風 (16-bit Pixel Art Sprite)
- **視覺特徵**：16-bit / 32-bit 點陣像素表情包、復古 JRPG 遊戲對話框頭像、俐落像素塊狀邊緣、懷舊街機與掌機感。
- **Prompt 核心關鍵字**：
  ```text
  Nostalgic 16-bit pixel art character sprite sticker, retro JRPG chat emote style, capturing key recognizable features from the person in <Picture 1>. Crisp chunky pixel clusters, vibrant retro color palette, expressive retro arcade emote poses, game UI sticker aesthetics.
  ```

---

## 5. 搞笑魔性顏藝 / 迷因風 (Meme & Caricature)
- **視覺特徵**：誇張的五官變形、爆笑魔性肢體動作、草根手繪塗鴉線條、LINE 爆笑貼圖與網路迷因表情包（例如崩潰、狂笑、翻白眼）。
- **Prompt 核心關鍵字**：
  ```text
  Humorous, funny exaggerated cartoon meme sticker, LINE sticker meme culture style, caricature based on the person in <Picture 1>. Hilarious exaggerated facial expressions, funny dynamic body comedy gestures, hand-drawn doodle lines, playful anime reaction face sticker aesthetics.
  ```

---

## 6. 韓系簡約文青手繪 (Korean Line & Pastel)
- **視覺特徵**：乾淨簡約的鋼筆/鉛筆線條、低飽和馬卡龍粉彩色系（Pastel）、慵懶生活治癒感、溫柔優雅插畫風。
- **Prompt 核心關鍵字**：
  ```text
  Minimalist Korean warm line-drawing sticker, cozy lifestyle aesthetic, delicate stylized illustration of the person in <Picture 1>. Smooth continuous pen lines, soothing muted pastel watercolor fills, charming gentle vibe, aesthetic daily chat sticker look.
  ```

---

## 7. 野獸派狂野風格 (Fauvism Wild Expression)
- **視覺特徵**：強烈飽和的非自然撞色（鮮烈朱紅、電光鈷藍、熾熱鎘黃、野性翠綠）、大膽放肆的狂野筆觸、馬蒂斯（Matisse）與德蘭（Derain）式的藝術張力與誇張面部表情。
- **Prompt 核心關鍵字**：
  ```text
  Wild Fauvism modern chat sticker illustration, inspired by Henri Matisse and André Derain. Bold, expressive, wild, and spontaneous brushstrokes, vibrant saturated clashing colors (fiery vermilion red, bright cadmium yellow, electric cobalt blue, wild emerald green, deep violet contours). Artistic, highly expressive character caricature capturing the recognizable facial features of the person in <Picture 1>. Dynamic thick artistic outlines, raw painterly brush textures, energetic and passionate emotional expression, modern pop-art chat sticker aesthetics.
  ```
